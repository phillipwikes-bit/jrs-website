### Why the silence was not a demand signal

A diagnosis dated 13 August 2026 found three causes, in the emails themselves:

1. **They were told not to reply.** All three closed with *"no reply needed."* Three non-responses to an email that asked nothing and waived a reply is the email working as written.
2. **The ask did not match the referral.** Broida's opening was *"having you as a trainer."* The emails offered free guides and a training link. **Nobody asked to be a trainer** — the door he opened was never walked through.
3. **Two of three went to a general inbox** rather than the named person.

Rewritten follow-ups were prepared on 13 August: six lines each, named recipient, one ask — a trainer slot — and no repeat of the free-materials pitch.

### The closure — 23 August 2026

**They were never sent.** Owner's determination:

> **"They are not at all interested, Broida was just blowing me off."**

The channel was closed. `scripts/close_referral_channel.py` removed the referrals from all three forward-looking plans and marked both outreach drafts **WITHDRAWN — DO NOT SEND**.

**The history was deliberately preserved**, on the stated principle that *a tracker that deletes its own history is worthless*, and because the record of a closed channel is what stops it being reopened by accident.

**The cost was stated rather than absorbed:** removing the referrals cost roughly 15 points. Training or curriculum engagement within 12 months moved from 35–50% down to **20–30%**, and paid engagement within 6 months from 55–70% down to **40–55%**.
